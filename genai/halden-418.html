A short, fictional docudrama about a small-plane engine fire and a field landing. Every picture shot is AI video, and every camera move was decided in 3D first.

Each shot starts as a board, gets blocked in Blender with a real camera, and the AI shot has to follow that camera. Left to right: sketch, layout, final.

Fixing a shot without regenerating it

When a shot is right except for one thing, I edit that one thing into the existing clip, so the plane, the camera and the timing stay locked.

The film

Review

Every shot went through Reel Room, my own review tool: versions per step, department approvals, draw-over notes and a full-cut player. One pass of every shot, then notes, then one batched redo.

Its project builder makes the folder tree and a Blender layout file per shot, with that shot’s camera, frame range and linked assets: download the project builder (Python + Blender, 32 KB).
